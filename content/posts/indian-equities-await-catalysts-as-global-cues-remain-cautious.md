## Market Overview
The Indian stock market has been trading in a range-bound manner over the past few sessions, with the Nifty 50 and Sensex indices struggling to break out of their respective resistance levels. Today's session was no different, with the market witnessing a mix of buying and selling interest across various sectors. The mood remains cautious, with investors awaiting cues from the upcoming monetary policy meeting and global economic developments. The Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) decision on interest rates is expected to have a significant impact on the market, with a rate hike likely to lead to a correction in equity prices. On the other hand, a status quo on rates could lead to a rally in the market.

The market has been driven by foreign institutional investor (FII) flows, which have been positive over the past few sessions. However, the flows have been volatile, with FIIs selling heavily in certain sessions. The domestic institutional investor (DII) flows have been steady, providing support to the market. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has been keeping a close eye on the market, with the regulator taking steps to curb excessive speculation and maintain market stability. The Indian economy has been performing well, with the gross domestic product (GDP) growth rate expected to be around 7% in the current fiscal year.

The global macro-economic developments have been having a significant impact on the Indian stock market. The US Federal Reserve's decision on interest rates has been influencing the market, with a rate hike likely to lead to a strengthening of the US dollar and a subsequent outflow of funds from emerging markets like India. The European Central Bank's (ECB) decision on interest rates has also been having an impact on the market, with a rate hike likely to lead to a strengthening of the euro and a subsequent outflow of funds from emerging markets. The trade tensions between the US and China have been having a significant impact on the global economy, with the Indian stock market being no exception.
