## Market Overview
The Indian stock market wrapped up the second quarter of 2026 on a cautious note, with the Nifty 50 and Sensex indices exhibiting volatility throughout the session. The mood was largely dictated by global macro-economic developments, as concerns over a potential slowdown in major economies weighed on investor sentiment. Despite the cautious tone, the Nifty 50 managed to hold above the 17,000 mark, while the Sensex hovered around the 57,000 level. The session was marked by a lack of conviction among market participants, as they awaited clarity on the earnings front and the direction of global economic trends.

The broader market painted a mixed picture, with the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 indices trading in a narrow range. The advance-decline ratio was marginally in favor of the decliners, indicating a subdued tone among market participants. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) continued to be net sellers, albeit at a slower pace, while domestic institutional investors (DIIs) provided support to the market. The net selling by FIIs was largely seen in the IT and Pharma sectors, while DIIs were net buyers in the Banking and Auto sectors.

The volatility in the market was also reflected in the India VIX, which rose by over 2% during the session. The increase in the India VIX indicates that market participants are pricing in higher volatility in the near term, which could be a function of the uncertainty surrounding global economic growth and its potential impact on domestic earnings. As the market heads into the earnings season, investors will be closely watching the performance of index heavyweights, particularly in the Banking and IT sectors, for cues on the direction of the market.

## Top Market Movers
* Infosys Ltd (INFY) was among the top gainers, rising by 1.5% to ₹1,432.50, on the back of positive commentary from brokerage firms ahead of its quarterly earnings.
* HDFC Bank Ltd (HDFCBANK) gained 1.2% to ₹1,542.10, as investors bet on the bank's ability to deliver strong earnings growth in the coming quarter.
* On the other hand, Tata Motors Ltd (TATAMOTORS) was among the top losers, declining by 2.1% to ₹444.50, due to concerns over the potential impact of a global slowdown on its Jaguar Land Rover business.
* Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (DRREDDY) fell by 1.8% to ₹4,321.10, as the Pharma sector witnessed selling pressure on concerns over regulatory issues and pricing pressure in the US market.
* Larsen & Toubro Ltd (LT) rose by 1.1% to ₹2,141.40, on the back of positive commentary from brokerage firms on its order book and execution capabilities.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The Banking sector was among the top performers, with the Nifty Bank index rising by 0.8% during the session. The sector was led by gains in HDFC Bank Ltd (HDFCBANK) and ICICI Bank Ltd (ICICIBANK), which rose by 1.2% and 1.1%, respectively. The IT sector, on the other hand, was among the top losers, with the Nifty IT index declining by 0.5% during the session. The sector was weighed down by losses in Infosys Ltd (INFY) and Wipro Ltd (WIPRO), which fell by 0.2% and 0.5%, respectively. The Auto sector was also in focus, with the Nifty Auto index rising by 0.3% during the session, led by gains in Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (MARUTI) and Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd (M&M).
