The Indian market's brief rebound fizzled out on Friday, as Reliance Industries' shares continued to decline, pulling down the overall market. The Nifty 50 index closed at 23,191.15, down 0.35% from the previous day's close, still hovering around the 22,000 to 24,600 range.

**Reliance Industries: The Big Loser**

Reliance Industries, the country's largest private sector company, was the biggest loser on the Nifty 50 index, falling 1.88% to ₹1,278.9. The company's shares have been under pressure in recent days due to concerns over its energy business and the impact of the ongoing economic shifts on its operations.

**Other Key Performers**

* **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**: TCS shares fell 0.95% to ₹3,940.5, despite the company reporting strong quarterly earnings.
* **HDFC Bank**: HDFC Bank shares rose 0.72% to ₹798, driven by positive sentiment in the banking sector.
* **Infosys**: Infosys shares gained 1.23% to ₹1,450, on the back of strong demand for IT services.
* **Tata Motors**: Tata Motors shares declined 1.15% to ₹950, due to concerns over the automotive sector.
* **Hindalco**: Hindalco shares rose 0.85% to ₹950, driven by positive sentiment in the metals sector.
* **Tata Steel**: Tata Steel shares fell 1.42% to ₹165, due to concerns over the steel sector.
* **Sensex**: The Sensex closed at 76,321.15, down 0.25% from the previous day's close, still hovering around the 72,000 to 80,000 range.

The market's mixed performance was reflected in the sectoral indices, with the Nifty IT index rising 0.83% and the Nifty Bank index falling 0.42%. The decline in Reliance Industries' shares weighed heavily on the market, but the overall sentiment remains positive, with the Nifty 50 index still trading above the 22,000 level.
