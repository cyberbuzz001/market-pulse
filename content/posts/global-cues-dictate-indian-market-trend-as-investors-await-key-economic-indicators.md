## Market Overview
The Indian stock market has been witnessing a significant amount of volatility in recent sessions, with the Nifty 50 index oscillating between 22,000 and 24,600 levels. Today's session was no exception, as the index traded in a narrow range, reacting to global cues and domestic economic developments. The market mood remains cautious, with investors awaiting key economic indicators, including the inflation rate and industrial production data, which are scheduled to be released later this week. The Sensex, on the other hand, has been trading in tandem with the Nifty, with the index hovering around the 75,000 mark.

The recent trend in the Indian stock market has been influenced by global macro-economic developments, particularly the monetary policy decisions of major central banks. The US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged has led to a rally in global equity markets, with the Indian market being no exception. However, the domestic economic concerns, including the rising inflation rate and sluggish industrial production, have capped the gains. The foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in the Indian market, with a net inflow of Rs 1,200 crore in the last trading session, according to the data from the National Stock Exchange (NSE).

The market participants are also closely watching the movement of the Indian rupee, which has been trading around the 82 levels against the US dollar. The rupee's movement has a significant impact on the Indian stock market, particularly on the IT and pharmaceutical sectors, which have a significant exposure to foreign currencies. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has been intervening in the currency market to stabilize the rupee, which has helped to calm the nerves of investors.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The banking sector has been one of the top performers in today's session, with the Bank Nifty index trading above the 38,000 levels. The sector has been driven by the positive sentiment surrounding the Indian economy, particularly the expectation of a rate cut by the RBI in its upcoming monetary policy meeting. The IT sector has also been trading higher, with the Nifty IT index up by over 1%. The sector has been driven by the positive earnings reported by some of the major IT companies, including Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) at ₹4,000 and Infosys at ₹1,500.

The pharmaceutical sector, on the other hand, has been trading lower, with the Nifty Pharma index down by over 1%. The sector has been impacted by the regulatory issues surrounding some of the major pharmaceutical companies, including Sun Pharmaceutical Industries and Dr. Reddy's Laboratories. The auto sector has also been trading lower, with the Nifty Auto index down by over 1%. The sector has been impacted by the sluggish sales reported by some of the major auto companies, including Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors at ₹950.
