## Market Overview
The Indian stock market witnessed a volatile session today, with the Nifty 50 index oscillating between gains and losses before finally settling above the crucial 22,000 level. The benchmark index closed at 22,042.15, up 0.2% from its previous close, as investors digested a mix of global and domestic cues. The BSE Sensex, meanwhile, ended the day at 73,349.41, up 0.1% from its previous close. Market sentiment remained cautious, with investors closely watching global economic developments and their potential impact on Indian equities. The rupee's movement against the US dollar also remained a key focus area, with the currency trading at 82.50 against the greenback.

The market mood was influenced by global growth concerns, with investors weighing the potential impact of a slowing global economy on Indian exports and corporate earnings. However, domestic economic resilience, driven by a robust services sector and government spending, helped limit the downside. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the market, offloading stocks worth Rs 1,200 crore, while domestic institutional investors (DIIs) provided support, buying stocks worth Rs 800 crore. The market breadth was narrow, with 1,044 stocks advancing and 1,011 stocks declining on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).

The Indian market's performance was also influenced by the recent trends in the global economy. The US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged and the European Central Bank's (ECB) decision to hike rates by 25 basis points had a mixed impact on global markets. While the US decision was seen as a positive for emerging markets, the ECB's move was viewed as a negative for risk assets. The Indian market, however, has been relatively resilient, with the Nifty 50 index outperforming its global peers in recent times.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The information technology (IT) sector was among the top performers today, with stocks like Infosys and TCS gaining ground. The sector's outperformance was driven by a strong demand for digital services and a weak rupee, which makes Indian IT services more competitive in the global market. The banking sector, however, underperformed, with stocks like HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank declining. The sector's weakness was driven by concerns over asset quality and the potential impact of a slowing economy on credit growth. The pharmaceutical sector was another underperformer, with stocks like Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (SUNPHARMA) and Cipla Ltd (CIPLA) declining.

## Technical Levels to Watch
The Nifty 50 index is facing resistance at the 22,500 level, which is a crucial hurdle for the bulls. A breach of this level could lead to a rally towards the 23,000 level. On the downside, the index has support at the 21,500 level, which is a key level for the bears. A breach of this level could lead to a decline towards the 21,000 level. The Bank Nifty index, meanwhile, is facing resistance at the 38,000 level and has support at the 36,500 level. The index is forming a bullish chart pattern, with a potential target of 40,000.
