## Market Overview
The Indian stock market witnessed a significant rebound today, with the Nifty 50 index rising by 1.2% to close at 23,500. The Sensex, meanwhile, gained 1.1% to end the day at 78,500. The market mood was bullish, driven by a recovery in global markets and expectations of strong Q1 earnings from key sectors such as IT and banking. The rebound was also fueled by a surge in foreign institutional investor (FII) flows, which have been net buyers in the Indian market over the past week. According to data from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), FIIs have invested a net of ₹15,000 crore in the Indian market so far this month.

The market breadth was positive, with 1,300 stocks advancing on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) compared to 650 decliners. The NSE's India VIX index, which measures volatility, fell by 2.5% to 15.20, indicating a decrease in market uncertainty. The rupee, meanwhile, strengthened against the dollar, closing at 81.20 per dollar. The strong rebound in the market has raised hopes of a sustained recovery, but investors remain cautious ahead of the Q1 earnings season, which kicks off next week. Companies such as Infosys (INFY), Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), and HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK) are expected to report strong earnings, which could further boost market sentiment.

The global macro-economic environment remains uncertain, with the US Federal Reserve expected to raise interest rates again next month. However, the Indian market has been resilient, driven by strong domestic demand and a recovery in key sectors such as manufacturing and construction. The government's focus on infrastructure development and Ease of Doing Business has also boosted investor sentiment. According to a report by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), the Indian economy is expected to grow at 7.5% this fiscal year, driven by a rebound in private consumption and investment.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The IT sector was the top performer today, with the NSE's IT index rising by 2.5%. Companies such as Infosys (INFY), Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), and Wipro (WIPRO) were among the top gainers. The banking sector also performed well, with the NSE's Bank index rising by 1.8%. Companies such as HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK), ICICI Bank (ICICIBANK), and Axis Bank (AXISBANK) were among the top gainers. The auto sector, meanwhile, was the worst performer, with the NSE's Auto index falling by 0.5%. Companies such as Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS) and Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M) were among the top losers.

The pharma sector also performed well, with the NSE's Pharma index rising by 1.2%. Companies such as Sun Pharmaceutical (SUNPHARMA) and Cipla (CIPLA) were among the top gainers. The metal sector, meanwhile, was flat, with the NSE's Metal index rising by 0.2%. Companies such as Tata Steel (TATASTEEL) and Hindalco (HINDALCO) were among the top gainers.
