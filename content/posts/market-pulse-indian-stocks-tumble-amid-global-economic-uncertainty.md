**Market Update:**

The Indian stock market has taken a hit today, with the Nifty 50 index plummeting by 1.5% as of 10:33 AM, currently hovering around 23,500. The decline is largely attributed to the global economic uncertainty, which has led to a surge in risk aversion among investors. The key indices, including the Nifty 50 and the Sensex, which is currently around 77,500, have fallen sharply, with TCS, Reliance Industries, and HDFC Bank being among the top losers.

**TCS:**

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) has been one of the worst performers today, with its stock price declining by 1.55% to ₹3,942. The company's share price has been under pressure due to concerns over the global economic slowdown and the impact of potential recession on its business. The stock's trading volume has increased significantly, with over 2.34 million shares changing hands.

**Reliance Industries:**

Reliance Industries has also seen a significant decline in its stock price, falling by 1.2% to ₹1,320. The company's shares have been affected by the overall market sentiment, as well as concerns over the impact of global economic uncertainty on its refining and petrochemical businesses.

**HDFC Bank:**

HDFC Bank's stock price has declined by 1.1% to ₹820, as investors become increasingly cautious about the banking sector's exposure to the global economic slowdown. The bank's shares have been under pressure due to concerns over the potential impact of a recession on its loan book and profitability.

**Other Losers:**

Other major losers include Infosys, which has fallen by 1.4% to ₹1,520, and Tata Motors, which has declined by 1.6% to ₹980. Hindalco has also seen a significant decline, falling by 1.3% to ₹950, while Tata Steel has dropped by 1.5% to ₹175. The market is expected to remain volatile in the coming days, as investors continue to grapple with the implications of global economic uncertainty.
