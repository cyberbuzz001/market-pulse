Indian equities continued their downward trajectory on Monday, with the Nifty 50 index shedding 0.5% to close at 23,100. The Sensex, too, declined by 0.4% to end at 77,500. The losses were led by the IT and banking sectors, which were hit by concerns over global economic growth and rising interest rates.

**Key Market Drivers:**

* **Global Economic Concerns:** The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has cut its global economic growth forecast for 2026, citing rising inflation and interest rates. This has led to increased uncertainty among investors, who are now more cautious in their investment decisions.
* **Rising Interest Rates:** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has raised interest rates for the fifth time in a row, to 6.5%. This has made borrowing more expensive for consumers and businesses, leading to a decline in demand. Notably, stocks like Reliance Industries, which closed at ₹1,350, and HDFC Bank, which ended at ₹820, were among the major losers, reflecting the bearish sentiment in the market. In the IT sector, TCS declined to ₹4,000, while Infosys closed at ₹1,500. On the other hand, metal stocks like Tata Steel, which closed at ₹170, and Hindalco, which ended at ₹950, also felt the pressure. Additionally, Tata Motors declined to ₹950, reflecting the overall gloom in the automotive sector.
