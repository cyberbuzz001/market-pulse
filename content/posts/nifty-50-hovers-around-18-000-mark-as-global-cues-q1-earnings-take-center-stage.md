## Market Overview
The Indian stock market has been witnessing a seesaw battle between bulls and bears, with the Nifty 50 index hovering around the crucial 18,000 mark. The ongoing Q1 earnings season has been a key driver of market sentiment, with investors closely watching the performance of index heavyweights such as Reliance Industries (RELIANCE) and HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK). Global macro-economic developments, including the US Federal Reserve's monetary policy decisions and the European Central Bank's actions, have also been influencing investor sentiment. The Indian rupee's movement against the US dollar has been another factor affecting the market, with a strong currency boosting investor confidence.

The market mood has been cautious, with investors adopting a wait-and-watch approach ahead of key economic data releases and the upcoming monetary policy meeting of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The RBI's decision on interest rates will be closely watched, as it is expected to have a significant impact on the market. Meanwhile, foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net sellers in the Indian market, while domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have been net buyers, providing some support to the market. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has also been taking steps to boost investor confidence and improve market transparency.

The Nifty 50 index has been trading in a narrow range, with support around 17,800 and resistance at 18,200. A breach of either level could trigger a significant move in the market. The Bank Nifty index has also been trading in a range, with support at 40,500 and resistance at 41,500. The market is expected to remain volatile in the near term, with investors closely watching global cues and domestic economic data releases. Companies such as Infosys (INFY) and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) are expected to release their Q1 earnings soon, which could have a significant impact on the market.
