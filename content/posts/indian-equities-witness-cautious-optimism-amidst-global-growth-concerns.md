## Market Overview
The Indian stock market witnessed a volatile session today, with the Nifty 50 index oscillating between 22,500 and 24,000 levels. The benchmark index ultimately settled around 23,000, reflecting a cautious optimism among investors. The broader market sentiment was influenced by global growth concerns, as the International Monetary Fund (IMF) lowered its global economic growth forecast for 2026. However, the domestic economic indicators, such as the recent uptick in manufacturing PMI and improving consumer sentiment, helped mitigate the negative impact of global news.

The market mood was further buoyed by the recent announcement from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to relax norms for foreign portfolio investors (FPIs), which is expected to attract more foreign capital into the Indian markets. The foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in the Indian markets over the past few weeks, with a net inflow of over ₹10,000 crores. This trend is expected to continue, given the attractive valuations of Indian stocks compared to their global peers. The domestic institutional investors (DIIs), on the other hand, have been net sellers, with a net outflow of over ₹5,000 crores, as they continue to book profits in the recent rally.

The sectoral rotation was evident today, with the IT and pharmaceutical sectors outperforming the broader market. The Nifty IT index gained over 1%, led by stocks like Infosys (₹1,500) and Tata Consultancy Services (₹4,000). The pharmaceutical sector also saw a significant uptick, with stocks like Sun Pharmaceutical and Cipla gaining over 2%. The banking sector, however, was a laggard, with the Nifty Bank index declining over 0.5%, led by stocks like State Bank of India and ICICI Bank. Other notable stocks include Reliance Industries (₹1,350), HDFC Bank (₹800), Tata Motors (₹950), Hindalco (₹950), and Tata Steel (₹170).
