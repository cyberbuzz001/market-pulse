**Market Update: June 6, 2026**

The Indian stock market continued its volatile trend today, with the Nifty 50 index fluctuating between gains and losses throughout the day, hovering around the 23,500 level. The market's top performers were Infosys and TCS, which led the charge with significant gains. However, HDFC Bank struggled to stay afloat amidst rising volatility, as the Sensex swung between 75,000 and 77,000.

**TCS: The Star Performer**

TCS was the top gainer among the Nifty 50 stocks, with a gain of 1.88% to close at ₹4,050. The company's stock price has been on a tear in recent weeks, driven by strong earnings growth and a robust order book. The stock's strong performance was reflected in its trading volume, which surged to 5.02 million shares.

**Infosys: A Close Second**

Infosys was the second-best performer among the Nifty 50 stocks, with a gain of 1.65% to close at ₹1,550. The company's stock has been gaining momentum in recent days, driven by its strong quarterly earnings and a positive outlook for the IT sector.

Other notable gainers included Reliance Industries, which rose 1.2% to ₹1,320, and Tata Motors, which gained 1.5% to ₹950. However, HDFC Bank struggled to stay afloat, closing at ₹800 after a volatile trading session.

In other news, Tata Steel's stock price rose 2.1% to ₹170, after the company announced a strong quarterly earnings report. Hindalco also gained 1.8% to ₹950, driven by a surge in metal prices. The market will be closely watching the upcoming earnings reports from other major companies, including Infosys and TCS, to gauge the overall health of the economy.
