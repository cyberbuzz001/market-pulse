## Market Overview
The Indian stock market is witnessing a cautious trading session, with the Nifty 50 index hovering around its crucial resistance level of 24,000. The market mood is subdued, reflecting the prevailing global macro-economic uncertainty and the recent outflows of foreign institutional investors (FIIs). The Sensex, too, is trading in a narrow range, unable to muster the momentum to cross its psychological barrier of 78,000. As the session progresses, market participants are closely watching the movement of key indices, as a breach of these levels could set the tone for the rest of the week.

The recent trend of FII outflows has been a major concern for market participants, with net sales of over ₹10,000 crores in the last week alone. This has put pressure on the Indian rupee, which has been depreciating against the US dollar. The depreciation of the rupee has, in turn, affected the profitability of export-oriented companies, particularly in the IT and pharmaceutical sectors. On the other hand, domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have been net buyers, providing some support to the market. The DIIs have been investing heavily in the banking and financial services sector, which has been a major driver of the market in recent times.

The global macro-economic environment, too, is playing a significant role in shaping the market trend. The US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged has led to a rally in the global markets, but the Indian market has been unable to capitalize on this positive sentiment. The European Central Bank's decision to hike interest rates has also added to the uncertainty, making investors cautious about their investments. As the market navigates through these challenges, investors are advised to remain vigilant and keep a close eye on the key indices and sectoral trends.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The banking sector has been the major outperformer of the day, with all the major banks trading in the green. The sector has been driven by the recent rise in bond yields, which has improved the net interest margins of banks. The IT sector, too, has been in focus, with companies like Infosys and TCS trading higher. However, the metal sector has been under pressure, with companies like Tata Steel and JSW Steel trading lower due to concerns over global demand.

The pharmaceutical sector has also been in the news, with companies like Sun Pharmaceutical and Dr. Reddy's Laboratories trading higher. The sector has been driven by the recent rise in crude oil prices, which has improved the profitability of pharmaceutical companies. The auto sector, too, has been in focus, with companies like Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors trading higher.

## Technical Levels to Watch
The Nifty 50 index is facing a key resistance at 24,200, which is a crucial level to watch out for. A breach of this level could lead to a rally towards 24,600, while a failure to cross this level could result in a decline towards 23,800. The Bank Nifty index, too, is facing a key resistance at 72,500, which is a crucial level to watch out for. A breach of this level could lead to a rally towards 73,500, while a failure to cross this level could result in a decline towards 71,500.

The chart patterns, too, are indicating a cautious trend. The Nifty 50 index is forming a head and shoulder pattern, which is a bearish pattern. The Bank Nifty index, too, is forming a similar pattern, which is indicating a bearish trend. However, the relative strength index (RSI) is indicating that the market is oversold, which could lead to a bounce back.
