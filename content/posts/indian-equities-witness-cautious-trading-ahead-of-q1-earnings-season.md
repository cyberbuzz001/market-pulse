## Market Overview
The Indian stock market witnessed a subdued trading session, with the Nifty 50 index oscillating between 17,800 and 17,900 levels. The benchmark index ultimately closed at 17,850, up 0.2% from its previous close. The S&P BSE Sensex also ended the day with marginal gains, rising 0.1% to 59,900. Market participants remained cautious, as they await the start of the Q1 earnings season, which is expected to provide clarity on the performance of Indian companies. The earnings reports from heavyweights such as Infosys Ltd. (INFY) and HDFC Bank Ltd. (HDFCBANK) will be closely watched, as they are likely to set the tone for the rest of the season.

The market mood was also influenced by global macro-economic developments, particularly the recent interest rate hikes by the US Federal Reserve. The Indian rupee, which has been under pressure in recent weeks, depreciated further against the US dollar, touching 82.50 levels. This has raised concerns about the impact of higher import costs on Indian companies, particularly those in the manufacturing sector. However, the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) decision to raise the repo rate by 25 basis points has helped to stabilize the currency, and market participants are hopeful that the central bank will continue to take measures to support the economy.

The Indian stock market has been witnessing a sectoral rotation in recent weeks, with investors shifting their focus from defensive sectors such as pharmaceuticals and consumer goods to cyclical sectors such as banking and finance. This trend is expected to continue, as the economy shows signs of recovery and interest rates remain high. The Nifty Bank index, which has been outperforming the broader market, rose 0.5% to 41,200, led by gains in stocks such as ICICI Bank Ltd. (ICICIBANK) and Axis Bank Ltd. (AXISBANK).

## Top Market Movers
* Larsen & Toubro Ltd. (LT) rose 2.5% to 2,150, on the back of strong order inflows and a positive outlook for the infrastructure sector.
* Tata Motors Ltd. (TATAMOTORS) gained 2.2% to 450, as the company's subsidiary, Jaguar Land Rover, reported a significant increase in sales.
* Hindalco Industries Ltd. (HINDALCO) fell 1.8% to 410, due to concerns about the impact of higher aluminum prices on the company's margins.
* Bharti Airtel Ltd. (BHARTIARTL) rose 1.5% to 750, as the company announced a significant increase in its subscriber base.
* Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. (SUNPHARMA) fell 1.2% to 950, due to concerns about the impact of US FDA inspections on the company's exports.

## Sectoral Spotlight
The banking sector was the top performer, with the Nifty Bank index rising 0.5% to 41,200. The sector was led by gains in stocks such as ICICI Bank Ltd. (ICICIBANK) and Axis Bank Ltd. (AXISBANK), which rose 1.2% and 1.1%, respectively. The IT sector also performed well, with the Nifty IT index rising 0.3% to 28,500, led by gains in stocks such as Infosys Ltd. (INFY) and Tata Consultancy Services Ltd. (TCS), which rose 0.8% and 0.5%, respectively. The metal sector, however, underperformed, with the Nifty Metal index falling 0.5% to 5,500, due to concerns about the impact of higher input costs on the sector.
