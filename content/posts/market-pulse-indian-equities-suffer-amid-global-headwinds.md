The Indian stock market, as represented by the NSE, suffered a decline today, with the benchmark indices falling by 1.28% and 1.22% respectively, with the Nifty 50 hovering around 23,500. The TCS-EQ and RELIANCE-EQ stocks led the decline, with prices falling by 2.5% and 1.8% respectively.

**Key Market Drivers:**

*   **TCS-EQ:** The stock fell by 95 points, or 2.5%, to close at ₹3,920. The decline was driven by a decrease in trade volume, with 3.88 million shares changing hands.
*   **RELIANCE-EQ:** The stock fell by 22 points, or 1.8%, to close at ₹1,280. The decline was driven by a decrease in trade volume, with 7.23 million shares changing hands.
*   **HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK):** The stock also saw a decline, falling by 1.2% to close at ₹795, amid concerns over interest rate hikes.
*   **Tata Steel (TATASTEEL):** The stock fell by 4.5% to close at ₹165, after the company's recent 10:1 stock split.
*   **Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS):** The stock declined by 2.1% to close at ₹945, due to weak sales numbers.
*   **Hindalco:** The stock fell by 1.9% to close at ₹940, amid concerns over global demand.
*   **Infosys (INFY):** The stock declined by 1.5% to close at ₹1,450, due to a decrease in trade volume.

**Market Sentiment:**

The market sentiment remains cautious, with the Sensex hovering around 76,500, as investors await the outcome of the upcoming monetary policy meeting. The decline in the Nifty 50 and Sensex has led to a decrease in investor confidence, with many opting for a wait-and-watch approach.
