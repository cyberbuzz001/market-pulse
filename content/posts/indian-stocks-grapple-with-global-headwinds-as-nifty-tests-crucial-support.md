## Market Overview
The Indian stock market has been navigating turbulent waters in recent sessions, with the Nifty 50 index struggling to find its footing amidst a backdrop of global economic uncertainty. Today's session saw the index test its crucial support level, as investors remain cautious about the prospects of a global recession. The Sensex, too, has been mirroring the Nifty's movements, with both indices trading in a narrow range as market participants await the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) upcoming monetary policy decision. The RBI's stance on interest rates will be closely watched, as it is expected to have a significant impact on the trajectory of the Indian economy.

The market mood remains subdued, with foreign institutional investors (FIIs) continuing to adopt a cautious approach. According to data from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), FIIs have been net sellers in the Indian market over the past few sessions, which has added to the downward pressure on the indices. On the other hand, domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have been providing support, with many mutual funds and insurance companies continuing to invest in Indian equities. The dichotomy in the investment strategies of FIIs and DIIs has resulted in a volatile market, with investors grappling to make sense of the conflicting signals.

The Indian rupee, too, has been under pressure, with the currency depreciating against the US dollar in recent sessions. The weakness in the rupee has had a ripple effect on the stock market, with companies having significant imports or foreign currency debt facing the brunt of the currency fluctuation. As the global economic landscape continues to evolve, Indian companies will need to navigate the challenges posed by a strong US dollar and a potential slowdown in global trade.
