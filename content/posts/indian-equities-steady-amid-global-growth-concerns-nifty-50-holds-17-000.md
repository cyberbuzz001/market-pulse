## Market Overview
The Indian stock market witnessed a volatile session today, with the Nifty 50 index oscillating between gains and losses before finally closing flat at 17,050. The Sensex, on the other hand, ended the day with a marginal loss of 0.1% at 57,300. Market participants remained cautious, weighing the impact of global growth concerns on the domestic economy. The recent surge in crude oil prices and strengthening of the US dollar have raised worries about inflation and interest rates, affecting investor appetite for riskier assets.

Despite these global headwinds, Indian equities have shown remarkable resilience, thanks to the country's strong consumption story and the IT sector's robust growth. The Nifty IT index has been a top performer, with stocks like Infosys (INFY) and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) hitting new highs. The domestic consumption theme has also been a favorite among investors, with companies like Hindustan Unilever (HUL) and Nestle India (NESTLEIND) witnessing significant buying interest. However, the banking and financial services sector has been under pressure, with stocks like State Bank of India (SBIN) and ICICI Bank (ICICIBANK) facing selling pressure.

The market mood remains cautious, with investors closely watching the developments in the global economy and their potential impact on India. The recent comments from the US Federal Reserve Chairman have raised concerns about a potential rate hike, which could lead to a flight of foreign capital from emerging markets like India. However, the domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have been net buyers in the market, providing support to the indices. The foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have also been net buyers, although their participation has been muted in recent sessions.
