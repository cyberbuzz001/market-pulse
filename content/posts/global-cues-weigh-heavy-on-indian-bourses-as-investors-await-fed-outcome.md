## Market Overview
The Indian stock market has been witnessing a tumultuous ride over the past few sessions, with global cues playing a significant role in shaping the market sentiment. The Nifty 50 index has been oscillating between 22,000 and 24,600 levels, indicating a clear lack of direction among investors. The benchmark index has been under pressure due to the ongoing concerns over inflation, interest rates, and global economic growth. The selling pressure has been more pronounced in the banking and financial sectors, with stocks like HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK) at ₹750-₹850 and Reliance Industries (RELIANCE) at ₹1,250-₹1,400 bearing the brunt.

The market mood has been cautious, with investors adopting a wait-and-watch approach ahead of the Federal Reserve's monetary policy decision. The Fed's decision is expected to have a significant impact on the global markets, and investors are eagerly awaiting the outcome. The Indian rupee has also been under pressure, depreciating against the US dollar, which has added to the market woes. The foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net sellers in the Indian market, which has further exacerbated the situation. On the other hand, the domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have been net buyers, providing some support to the market.

The Indian stock market has been experiencing a high degree of volatility, with the India VIX index surging to its highest level in recent months. This indicates that investors are expecting a high degree of uncertainty in the market, and are pricing in the potential risks. The market participants are advised to exercise caution and maintain a disciplined approach to investing, as the market is expected to remain volatile in the near term. The investors should focus on stock-specific approach, rather than taking a broad-based approach, as the market is expected to throw up opportunities in specific stocks.
